# Verification Checklist

Use this checklist before an AI-assisted research output is treated as decision-ready.

## 1. Source coverage

- Confirm every source used in the synthesis is present and readable.
- Record which source supports each material claim.
- Do not treat missing or inaccessible material as evidence.

## 2. Evidence traceability

- Check each factual statement against its source.
- Preserve qualifiers such as "proposed," "reported," "may," or "not confirmed."
- Separate source facts from interpretation, recommendations, and open questions.

## 3. Conflict check

- Flag contradictions between sources instead of silently resolving them.
- Keep unresolved conflicts visible in the synthesis.
- Return to the evidence-validation stage when two sources cannot both support the same conclusion.

## 4. Uncertainty check

- Identify information that is missing, ambiguous, or weakly supported.
- Avoid converting possibilities into conclusions.
- Mark assumptions explicitly if they are necessary to continue analysis.

## 5. Human review

A person reviews the draft before approval and asks:

- Does the synthesis accurately represent the source material?
- Are important disagreements or caveats still visible?
- Did the AI introduce a claim that cannot be traced to evidence?
- Is the recommendation proportionate to the available evidence?
- Is more information needed before a decision is made?

## Revision triggers

Return the workflow to evidence validation when:

- a claim cannot be traced to a source
- a source contradicts the draft conclusion
- tentative language has become overly certain
- important context is missing
- the reviewer cannot explain why a recommendation follows from the evidence

## Approval standard

The output is decision-ready only when the human reviewer can trace material claims to evidence, understand remaining uncertainty, and knowingly approve the final interpretation.
