# Synthetic Workshop Research Example

All sources and outputs below are fictional and hand-authored for demonstration. They describe no real organization, participant, or project. This is not a model-run record or evidence of measured performance.

## Question and source intake

What needs resolving before a community team confirms a beginner digital-skills workshop?

- **S1, paragraph 1 — planning note:** "The team proposes a beginner digital-skills workshop. Registration instructions should use plain language."
- **S2, paragraph 1 — venue note:** "The meeting room is available on Thursday evening. Wi-Fi is available. Loaner laptops are not provided."
- **S3, paragraph 1 — facilitator note:** "I can facilitate on Tuesday evening, but I am unavailable on Thursday evening. A bring-your-own-device format may exclude people without a laptop."

Use this question and the three labeled sources with [the extraction prompt](../prompts/structured-extraction.md).

**Conflict:** S2 and S3, paragraph 1, describe incompatible room and facilitator availability for Thursday.

**Unanswered questions:** Budget, attendance, accessibility requirements, alternate venue dates, and device support are not stated in supplied sources.

**Status:** Pending evidence validation and human review.

## Thematic classification

- Scheduling: C3 and C6.
- Access and communication: C2, C5, and C7.
- Scope and infrastructure: C1 and C4.

These are organizational labels, not additional source facts.

## Evidence validation

Compare every excerpt with the fictional inputs. Preserve the scheduling conflict and tentative wording of C7. Reject "Thursday is confirmed": room availability does not establish a booking or facilitator availability.

## Synthesis: illustrative draft

The notes support planning a beginner workshop but do not support confirming Thursday. Resolve the scheduling conflict, clarify device support and Wi-Fi suitability, and prepare plain-language registration instructions. These are proposed actions, not completed work.

## Human review and revision loop

Check whether the draft implies a confirmed date or measured device demand. If so, return to validation, correct the unsupported claim, revise the synthesis, and review again. A real reviewer must decide approval; none is claimed here.

## Decision-ready format: illustrative only

- **Proposed decision:** Hold date confirmation until room and facilitator availability align (C3, C6).
- **Follow-up:** Clarify alternate dates, device support, and Wi-Fi suitability (C3–C7); prepare plain-language registration instructions (C2).
- **Open questions:** Budget, attendance, and accessibility requirements remain unknown.
- **Approval status:** Pending human review. This sample is not an approved operational decision.
